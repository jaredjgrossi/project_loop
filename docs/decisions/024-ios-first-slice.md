# 024 · iPhone app, first slice

*2026-11-04 · branch `ios-first-slice`*

## Decision
Build the iPhone app's main screen in SwiftUI (iOS 17+, iPhone only) in `ios/`, against the saved
route `ios/Fixtures/kips3-ui.json`, with no engine calls. The Xcode project is generated by
XcodeGen from `ios/project.yml` and is not committed. Map: Mapbox Maps SDK 11.34.2 (Swift Package
Manager, no secret token needed), Standard style configured quiet. Look Around: MapKit's
`MKLookAroundViewController`.

## Choices worth knowing
- **Numbers come from the file only.** `CardText` builds every sentence and label from decoded
  fields. The ranking score and the once-per-crosswalk lights value are not decoded at all, so
  they can't reach the screen. The card's lights figure is `summary.lights_per_mile` (every
  crossing, 3.6).
- **Reason sentence bands** (Jared, 2026-11-04): 60%+ waterfront "Mostly by the water", 40–59%
  "About half by the water", under 40% "Mostly on streets", then ": X lights a mile." The band is
  picked on the whole-number percent the card shows, so the sentence and the stat agree.
- **Route drawn from the file's pieces** (decision 023's route pieces). Each `"feature": "piece"`
  (waterfront, park, path, street) is drawn as its kind; park is drawn as path (dashed) for now.
  For drawing only, every piece under 20 m joins its longer neighbour (shortest first), then
  neighbours of the same kind join. On this route the 25 pieces draw as 11 runs: 42 m of short
  street bits on the esplanade draw as path (307 m of path drawn in all) and 5 m at the turn back
  joins the waterfront. The profile strip's bars use the same 11 runs (path as a dashed bar in
  route-path; "path" joins the legend only when the route has path).
- **Detail follows the zoom.** On the map, runs shorter than 40 pt on screen join their longer
  neighbour, and a path run is dashed only when it fits two full dashes (30 pt); shorter path
  runs are drawn solid in route-path. Recomputed as the camera zooms, in the same frame.
- **One continuous line at every zoom** (bug on Jared's phone, 2026-11-04: a gap below mile 1
  when zooming out). Cause: Mapbox's `line-dasharray` starts the 9/6 pattern at each run's start
  and stops at its end, so with butt caps a dashed run could end inside a 6 pt off-gap and show
  the casing as a hole; the phase changed with zoom. Fix: the casing is one line for the whole
  drawn route under every run; path dashes are cut as geometry, the pattern stretched or squeezed
  (at most half a period) so each run starts and ends on a dash; every run reaches 0.5 pt past
  each join (1 pt overlap, butt caps); casing and runs go in one source update, recomputed in
  the frame the zoom changes, so no frame shows a run missing. Checked frame by frame on a
  recorded slow pinch out over mile 1 (`PinchRecordingTests`, recording only).
- **Even width at every zoom** (bug on Jared's phone, 2026-11-06: lumpy, uneven edges, worst
  between mile 1 and the turnaround). Checked in order: GeoJSON simplification was not it
  (tolerance 0 changed nothing, kept anyway); runs were already cut from the route line's own
  141 vertices, not the 945 sample points; it is a Mapbox line layer. The cause is the route
  geometry: about 40 right-angle jogs of 4–10 m (crosswalk and path switches), dense on the
  esplanade and repeated on the way back. A jog narrower than the line can't be drawn at an even
  width, so it showed as a lump; the butt-capped, overlapping runs added notches where runs met
  at a jog. Fix: the casing and a solid base line (street style, round joins and caps) are one
  line each from the drawn geometry; waterfront and path are styled on top, cut edge to edge
  with butt caps and no overlap (path on a casing-coloured bed so dash gaps read as gaps); and
  the drawn line drops jogs under 2.5 pt at the current zoom (Douglas–Peucker over the whole line
  once, display only, recomputed with the zoom; close in every jog shows). The two legs of the
  out-and-back simplify as mirror images and stay on top of each other.
- **Selection by tapping the route** (Jared, 2026-11-04). A tap within 22 pt of the line, at any
  zoom, selects the nearest point on it (ties on an out-and-back go to the outbound leg): a small
  dot, a selection tick, the strip cursor moves there, and the chip reads "<stretch> · x.x mi ·
  Look around" with binoculars (stretch and distance only where Apple has no scene). Mile marks
  select that exact mile (30 pt numbered marker, same chip). Taps only, through Mapbox's tap
  recognizer, so drags and pans never select; a tap away from the line clears it. The sheet
  doesn't open on selection; the chip sits beside, above or below the marker and always above
  the sheet. Opening the sheet with nothing selected still selects mile 1.
- **Hidden labels.** Mile labels whose circle would sit under the selection marker or its chip
  are hidden. Markers never draw over the header.
- **Sheet motion.** The sheet's height is driven frame by frame (its own observable): 1:1 with the
  finger while dragging, resisting past either end, then a 320 ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)
  settle. The open content is always laid out and only clipped and faded. The map refits only
  after the sheet settles. The Mapbox logo and attribution ride 8 pt above the sheet on every
  frame; the attribution button is tinted ink-muted.
- **Whole-route stats.** The three stats and the crowd line describe the whole route.
  Selecting a mile only moves the marker, the chip and the profile cursor.
- **Crowd line names stretches** (Jared, 2026-11-04): "Crowds at 7:00 · moderate on E 31st St ·
  esplanade and FDR not scored · estimate". Only the busiest band present is named (high
  "busy", medium "moderate", low "quiet"), then the stretches without crowd data by bare name,
  longest first, two names at most per part. Two data lines, wrapped only at " · ". The signals
  line is dropped (it repeats the lights stat), so the open sheet stays at 398 pt.
- **No trust label the file doesn't give.** The #2 and #3 distances show the label from the
  file's `top3[].distance` object; a distance without one would show none.
- **Map framing.** The route is fitted below the provenance line and at least 40 pt above the
  sheet's top, refitted when the sheet opens or closes. Candidate outlines show only while
  building and fade out within 500 ms of the provisional flag.
- **Out-and-back mile labels.** When two marks land within 24 pt, each label moves off the line
  to the left of its direction of travel (outbound one side, return the other), with a leader
  and a dot at the exact point.
- **Debug only.** The "build" replay button and the launch-argument hooks exist only in Debug builds.
- **Build replay.** `BuildState` is a pure state machine over the three messages of decision 018.
  The route may change under the runner only before the provisional flag and only if they haven't
  touched the route or the card. Otherwise a "better route" chip appears; a new #1 after the flag
  also goes to the chip.
- **Night basemap.** Mapbox Standard runs on its "day" light preset in both themes so the map
  shows our token colors as given ("night" turns the Night land #1C1915 into about #020304).
  Under "day" the basemap's label halos stay cream and Standard has no halo color setting, so
  Night shows no basemap labels (neighborhood or street names). Our route layers ignore the
  basemap lighting. Night also hides Standard's footpaths (`showPedestrianRoads`): they draw as
  thick light lines that read as dashes, and Standard has no color setting for them.
- **Dashes mean path only: ferry lines masked.** Standard has no setting for its dashed ferry
  routes on the water. The app draws Mapbox Streets v8 water polygons over them in our water
  color, in Standard's middle slot.
- **Without a Mapbox token** the app draws on a plain paper background, so layout and route can
  be checked before the account exists.
- **Mapbox logo and attribution** stay visible just above the sheet (Mapbox terms).
- **App icon.** Single-size 1024 AppIcon with light, dark and tinted appearances, from the design
  folder unchanged; `project.yml` names it so `xcodegen` keeps it.

## Landing sheet (UI chat, 2026-11-09)
- **Sheet, not bar.** The floating request bar is replaced by a bottom sheet modeled on Apple
  Maps' home sheet: the request field (sunken, Fraunces placeholder; the launch typing happens
  here), "Your runs" tiles, and "Recent". It rises at launch and slides away on send, driven by
  the same frame-by-frame `SheetState` as the route card, so the Mapbox logo follows it.
- **Runs live on the phone.** `RunStore` keeps saved runs and recent routes in one JSON file in
  Application Support (no account). A recent route is the contract's `recent_routes` item (id,
  date, name, distance_mi, times_run) plus the request text and a simplified thumbnail shape,
  which never leave the phone. A first launch seeds "Usual" and one recent from the fixture's #1;
  every sent request that builds a route goes to the top of Recent.
- **Tiles.** A route's own shape (route-water line, vermilion start dot). Suggestions ("3 mi from
  here", "By the water") fill the row after the runner's own runs and send their request; with no
  route yet they draw a small glyph in the same style (a loop back to the dot; a line along a wavy
  shoreline), never an empty placeholder or a fake route. A saved run or recent row draws its
  route with no wait and opens its card.
- **Collapsible.** Three rests on the route card's `SheetState` (same 1:1 drag and 320 ms settle;
  no haptic, like the route card): collapsed to just the field, open (field, runs, first recent:
  how it arrives), and taller for more recents. Tapping the handle toggles; tapping the field
  while collapsed starts typing without opening the runs.
- **Weather.** WeatherKit needs the paid developer program; the build uses a Personal Team, so
  Debug builds show fixed sample weather (48°, partly cloudy) and Release builds hide the line.
- **Back home.** The card's ×, a swipe down past collapsed, or the wordmark clear the route and
  the sentence and bring the landing sheet back; the dismissed route moves to the top of Recent
  (without counting another run).
- **Type.** Only the design system's twelve type styles exist: the brief's without `data` and
  `data-strong`, plus `field` (Fraunces 18/24) and `numeral-s` (Fraunces 18/22). `TypeStyle`'s
  initializer is private to `Typography.swift`. The audit is in `docs/ios-type-audit.md`.
- **Calm pass on the route sheet (UI review, 2026-11-09).** No JetBrains Mono (font and licence
  removed). Trust words are hidden by default: measured values show none, estimates carry "≈",
  and tapping a number (or the crowd sentence, or a note) shows its trust word and the file's
  source in a popover; VoiceOver still reads the trust word. This keeps product rule 2 (every
  fact carries a source and a trust label) one tap away instead of on screen. The crowd line is one
  sentence, only when a stretch is moderate or busy. No strip legend. "How Loop read it" rows are
  plain words with no source words and no distance-tolerance row.
- **Mapbox logo and attribution** fade out while the route sheet is expanded past its open state,
  where the sheet covers the map.
- **The paper-model landing** (3D buildings, dawn light, 45° pitch) was tried on device and
  dropped; the flat map stays.

## Open
- Apple's MapKit terms for showing Look Around beside a non-Apple map: unchecked. Fine for a build
  on Jared's own phone; must be checked before any public release.
- Mapbox mobile pricing: 25,000 monthly active users free, then $4.00 per 1,000 (pricing page,
  2026-11-04). The page doesn't define how mobile users are counted.
- Night values for the four mockup-only colors (control border, request rule, grabber, unit ink)
  are first picks.
- Night has no neighborhood or street labels for this slice (Jared, 2026-11-04). A custom Mapbox
  Studio style comes later as a design task.
- Basemap neighborhood labels can sit under the Mapbox logo (East Village, with the sheet open);
  fine for now (Jared, 2026-11-04).
- The water mask sits above Standard's roads, so anything Standard draws over water below the
  labels is covered too: bridge decks would disappear (none on this route; the Brooklyn,
  Williamsburg and Queensboro bridges matter for later routes). Standard's white one-way arrows
  (zoomed in) have no setting either. Both go to the custom Mapbox Studio style.
- Drag stutter (Jared's phone, 2026-11-04): `SheetDragPerfTests` (XCTHitchMetric, 5 drag cycles,
  scheme LoopPerf) on the iPhone measured 0 hitches for the new sheet in Debug and in Release,
  and also 0 for the previous build, so synthetic drags don't reproduce what was felt. On the
  device by hand, Jared reports the reworked drag smooth (2026-11-04, Debug build).
- Dynamic Type: sizes are fixed; `TypeStyle.scale` is the one place scaling would plug in.
- Landing sheet height: open, the content (field, tiles with two label lines, one recent row)
  needs about 370 pt including the home indicator; the brief said about 262 pt. A new runner's
  (no recents) is about 290 pt; collapsed, about 120 pt.
- The Save tile isn't a button yet: what it saves from the landing is undecided.
- WeatherKit and its required Apple Weather attribution, once the paid program is in place.
- Mapbox's terms ask for the logo wherever the map shows. It's hidden only while "How Loop read
  it" covers the map; check this with Mapbox before any public release.
